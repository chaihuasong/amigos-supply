About Us

Ningbo Amigos Supply Chain Co., Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "Amigos") is a non-vessel operating common carrier (NVOCC) approved by China's Ministry of Transport.

Founded in 2018 and headquartered in Ningbo, China, we specialize in providing international express, international air freight, ocean freight (FCL/LCL/bulk cargo), and FBA overseas warehouse services.

With our professional team and extensive industry experience, we have established long-term partnerships with major shipping lines and airlines worldwide, enabling us to provide safe, fast, and cost-effective logistics solutions.
